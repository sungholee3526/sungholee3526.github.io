A Flutter based web-app for Bear Robotics employees taking the shuttles to
commute.

- Aggregates shuttle location and prediction data from two different
  operators(Commute.org, Stanford Margurite).
- Displays shuttles operated by different operators in one map view.

[https://bear-shuttle.web.app](https://bear-shuttle.web.app){:target="\_blank"}
